ABOUT THE ARTWORK

Rafaël Rozendaal — Rooms #0 (2026)

Rafaël Rozendaal is a Dutch-Brazilian artist known for digital and internet-based artworks. Rooms #0 uses bold geometric planes and vivid color to suggest an architectural space. The artwork is documented as on-chain generative JavaScript art on Ethereum. This interactive reconstruction invites you to explore its composition by arranging the individual planes.

Unofficial educational recreation based on the provided artwork documentation.

How to play

Select a colored plane, then drag it over the dark canvas. Use a two-finger trackpad scroll or the R key to rotate it. When it is close to its original position and orientation, it snaps into place.
